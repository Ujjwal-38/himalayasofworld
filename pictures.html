Annapurna
Annapurna, the world's tenth-highest peak at 8,091 meters, is located in Nepal. Known for its stunning beauty, it's also infamous for its high fatality rate among climbers.
Mount Everest
Mount Everest, the world's highest peak at 8,849 meters (29,032 feet), lies in the Himalayas on the Nepal-Tibet border.
Mount K2
K2, the world's second-highest peak at 8,611 meters, lies in the Karakoram Range between Pakistan and China. Known as the "Savage Mountain," it's famed for its beauty and challenging climbs.
Kanchenjunga
Kangchenjunga, the world's third-highest peak at 8,586 meters, lies between Nepal and Sikkim, India. Known as the "Five Treasures of Snow," it's celebrated for its beauty, sacred significance, and biodiversity.
Lhotse
Lhotse, the world's fourth-highest peak at 8,516 meters, is located near Mount Everest in the Himalayas. Renowned for its striking beauty, it's a climber's dream.
Makalu
Makalu, the world's fifth-highest peak at 8,485 meters, stands in Nepal. Known for its iconic pyramid shape, it's both stunning and challenging to climb.
Cho Oyu
Cho Oyu, the world's sixth-highest peak at 8,188 meters, lies on the Nepal-Tibet border. Known for its beauty and relatively easier ascent, it's a favorite among climbers.
Dhaulagiri
Dhaulagiri, the world's seventh-highest peak at 8,167 meters, stands in Nepal. Known as the "White Mountain," it's famed for its dazzling beauty and challenging climbs.
Manaslu
Manaslu, the world's eighth-highest peak at 8,163 meters, is located in Nepal. Known for its stunning landscapes and challenging climbs, it's a quieter alternative to Everest.
Nanga Parbat
Nanga Parbat, the ninth-highest peak at 8,126 meters, is located in Pakistan. Known as the "Killer Mountain" for its dangerous climbs, it offers breathtaking views and a formidable challenge.
